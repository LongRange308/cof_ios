Course of Fire — Support

Help for the iPhone app

Course of Fire is a stage builder, shot timer, and scoring tool for precision rifle, NRL22 / rimfire, and pistol / carbine shooting. It is developed by Michael Leaf.

Contact

Send a message with the form. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Screenshots, .cof stage files, PDFs, a short screen recording or a .zip. Up to 10 MB in total.

Microphone

The shot timer asks for microphone access so it can hear shots and record their times. Sound is analyzed on your iPhone only while the timer or the microphone test is running. Audio is not recorded, saved, or sent off the device.

If shot detection does not start, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Microphone, and turn Course of Fire on. You can also use the timer without the microphone. It then works as a stopwatch you stop yourself.

Your stages

Stages, range setup, timer settings, and scored runs stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone. The app has no account and no sign-in. Uninstalling the app deletes that data from the phone.

Sharing

Share, print, PDF, picture, CSV, and Course of Fire (.cof) files leave your iPhone only when you send them. The iPhone share sheet asks you where they should go.
